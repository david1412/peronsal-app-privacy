AGsafe Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 5, 2026

AGsafe is a private family vault application that helps users store personal records, passwords, documents, and household information in a secure and private way.

We respect the privacy of our users. The app is designed so that the app owner does not access user passwords, vault contents, or personal documents unless the user explicitly shares that information outside the app.

1. Information We Collect

AGsafe may collect and process the following information:

  • Email address for account creation, login, and password recovery.
  • Account password used to protect the user's personal vault.
  • Recovery hint used for account verification and recovery checks.
  • Vault entries such as login details, notes, contact information, and household records entered by the user.
  • Uploaded documents or files chosen by the user and stored in the app.
  • Authentication and session information needed to keep the user signed in securely.
  • Device and app information required to support secure access, app functionality, and system protection.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect the app with secure logins.
  • Support password reset and recovery features.
  • Store and retrieve the user's private vault records.
  • Allow users to save and access their documents in the app.
  • Protect the app from unauthorized use and misuse.
  • Provide the functionality required for the AGsafe service.

3. Data Storage and Protection

AGsafe stores user data in secure cloud services required to operate the app. Sensitive information is designed to be protected using encryption and secure access controls where supported by the app architecture.

Passwords, vault entries, and uploaded files are not intended to be stored in plain text for normal app use. We aim to keep personal information private and protect it using reasonable technical measures.

4. No Access to User Secrets

AGsafe is built as a private family vault application. We do not intentionally access, read, or view user passwords, saved secrets, household records, or uploaded documents.

Only the user account associated with the vault is intended to access that information. We do not sell or expose private vault content to third parties for advertising or unrelated purposes.

5. Data Sharing

AGsafe does not sell user personal data.

We do not use user data for advertising or marketing purposes.

We may share necessary data with trusted service providers that are required to operate the app, including secure hosting, authentication, and storage providers. These providers are used only to support the technical operation of AGsafe.

We do not share user vault contents or personal secrets with third parties for marketing, resale, or unrelated business purposes.

6. Device Features and Permissions

AGsafe may use device features such as:

  • Internet access for app functionality and cloud sync.
  • Biometric authentication for secure app access.
  • Document selection and file handling for uploaded household records and files.

These permissions are used only for the core functionality of AGsafe and are not used for unrelated tracking or profiling.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account data only for as long as needed to provide the AGsafe service and to fulfill legal or operational requirements.

If a user deletes their account, the app may remove associated vault data and uploaded files from the active service, subject to operational retention limits or necessary technical backups.

Users can request account deletion through the app or by contacting the developer using the information below.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information, including secure access controls, encrypted handling where supported, and protected authentication flows.

No online system is completely risk-free. We take reasonable steps to minimize the risk of unauthorized access, but no method of transmission or electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

AGsafe is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental consent where required by law.

10. Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information, but doing so may limit the ability of AGsafe to create or maintain your account and vault.

You may remove or delete vault entries, uploaded documents, and account data through the app, where supported.

11. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy over time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and notify users through reasonable means where required.
